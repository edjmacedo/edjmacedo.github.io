Support

Questions, a wrong word or clue, or an idea? Write to edvan.macedo.junior@gmail.com. Please say which game, language and day it was.

When do new puzzles come out?

Every day at midnight, in your phone's time zone. Each puzzle can be played once.

How do I change the language?

Tap the language button on the home screen, next to the date, or go to Settings → Language. The app and all its games switch to it. On the first start, the app uses your phone's language.

What are coins?

You earn coins by finishing the daily games, and you spend them on hints. Coins can't be bought.

I lost my coins or my stats

They are saved on your phone only, so deleting the app deletes them. A phone backup that includes app data brings them back on a new phone.
